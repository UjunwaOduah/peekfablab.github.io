Bluetooth speaker lamp

Created as a 2nd year MCT group project for the Fab Lab Experience class. The challenge was to make an interactive and functional product that used 3 core fablab skills: 3D printing, laser cutting, and Arduino. The end result for this group was a Bluetooth speaker lamp with a rainbow design.
